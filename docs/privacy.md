# Media Saver — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-03_

Media Saver does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- **No data leaves your browser to us.** The extension has no server, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party scripts.
- **What it reads.** On Instagram, X, TikTok and LinkedIn the extension reads the page you are viewing and the data the site has already loaded for that page, only to find the photo or video you click to download.
- **Network requests.** Files are downloaded directly from the site's own servers (for example cdninstagram.com, twimg.com, tiktokcdn.com, licdn.com). When a post's data is missing, the extension may make a single request to Instagram's own API for that post, exactly as the Instagram website does when you open a post. These requests use your existing session with the site and go only to that site.
- **Stored settings.** Your preferences (file name template, video quality, "ask where to save") are stored with `chrome.storage.sync` in your browser and synced by Chrome to your Google account if you have sync enabled. They are never sent anywhere else.
- **Downloads.** Downloaded files are saved by Chrome to your Downloads folder.
